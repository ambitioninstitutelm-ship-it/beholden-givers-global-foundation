Hamare Bare Mein (About Us)

Beholden Givers Global Foundation ek non-profit organization hai jo zarooratmand logon ki madad karne, samajik kalyan aur unki zindagi mein sakaratmak badlav lane ke liye samarpit hai. Hum sabhi ke liye ek behtar aur roshan bhavishya banane mein vishwas rakhte hain.

Hamare Karye (Our Focus Areas)

Shiksha (Education)

Bachon aur zarooratmandon ko unki padhai aur skill development mein sahayta dena.

Swasthya (Healthcare)

Swasthya shivir lagana aur health awareness failana.

Samajik Kalyan (Social Welfare)

Gareebon aur asahayon ki zaroori cheezon se sahayta karna.

Mahila Sashaktikaran

Mahilaon ko aatmanirbhar banane ke liye vibhinn karyakram chalana.
